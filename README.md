# Investment Policy Statement with Portfolio Models

An individual finance project that builds a full **Investment Policy Statement (IPS)** for a client portfolio with a healthcare-sector investment thesis, backed by a working Excel portfolio model.

Completed as an individual assignment in the **BBA Finance** program at Hult International Business School.

---

## The problem

A client needs a disciplined, documented framework for how their money will be invested, monitored, and rebalanced over time. The goal was to translate the client's objectives, risk tolerance, time horizon, and liquidity needs into a concrete asset-allocation and investment-selection strategy, with a clear thesis on where to concentrate growth exposure.

## The approach

- **Client profiling:** investment objectives, risk tolerance, time horizon, and liquidity needs translated into portfolio constraints.
- **Sector thesis:** a healthcare-sector overweight argued on defensive characteristics plus structural growth (aging demographics, AI/telemedicine, digital health post-pandemic).
- **Strategy design:** strategic asset allocation, a "sleeve" approach to organizing holdings, equity selection criteria, tax considerations, and a rebalancing policy.
- **Quantitative model:** an Excel portfolio model (Tech & SPY) with return forecasting to support allocation and performance-evaluation decisions.

## Deliverables

- [`Investment Policy Statement .docx`](Investment%20Policy%20Statement%20.docx) — the full IPS: purpose, duties, client profile, risk/return, asset allocation, investment philosophy, performance evaluation, rebalancing, and review policy.
- [`Basic Investment Pf Model_Tech & SPY_Forecasting adjusted-2.xlsx`](Basic%20Investment%20Pf%20Model_Tech%20&%20SPY_Forecasting%20adjusted-2.xlsx) — the supporting portfolio and forecasting model.
